🎮 How to Play & Setup

  • Step 1 (The Screen): One computer clicks "Host Laboratory Terminal" to show the master beaker on the classroom screen.
  • Step 2 (The Controls): Students open this file on their own phones/tablets, enter a name, type the 4-digit code from the host screen, and click "Enter Laboratory".
  • Step 3 (Secret Roles): You are assigned a role! **Chemists** must launch the correct atoms to build formulas. **Saboteurs** try to secretly ruin formulas or drop toxic waste!
  • Step 4 (Adapt): Look out for random temperature and recipe updates that shake up your elements without warning!
